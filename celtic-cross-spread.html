Celtic Cross Position Meanings

  • 1. Present: Your current situation and state of being
  • 2. Challenge: The immediate obstacle or challenge crossing your path
  • 3. Conscious: What you are aware of, your conscious goals
  • 4. Subconscious: Hidden influences, subconscious desires or fears
  • 5. Past: Recent past events that led to this situation
  • 6. Future: What is approaching in the near future
  • 7. Self: Your attitude, how you see yourself in this situation
  • 8. Environment: External influences, how others see you
  • 9. Hopes/Fears: Your hopes and fears about the outcome
  • 10. Outcome: The likely outcome if you continue on this path
